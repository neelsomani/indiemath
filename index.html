IndieMath

Community-Funded AI Mathematical Problem Solving

What We Do

IndieMath is a platform that enables the community to fund advanced AI attempts at solving open problems in mathematics and computer science. We leverage state-of-the-art AI models to tackle some of the most challenging unsolved problems in these fields.

Our Mission: Make cutting-edge mathematical research accessible and community-driven by harnessing the power of artificial intelligence.

How It Works

1. Problem Selection

We maintain a curated list of open mathematics and computer science problems. These range from computational complexity questions to unsolved conjectures in various mathematical domains.

2. Community Funding

Users contribute funds toward specific problems, choosing whether they want to fund attempts to prove or disprove a particular statement. Contributions directly fund compute time for advanced AI models.

3. AI Computation

Funded amounts are used to run state-of-the-art AI models (such as Claude with extended reasoning capabilities) against the selected problems. These computational sessions can run for extended periods as the AI explores potential solutions.

4. Review Process

When an AI proposes a solution, it undergoes rigorous human expert review to verify correctness. Only solutions that pass this verification process are accepted.

Our Services

  • Payment Processing: We accept monetary contributions via credit card and other payment methods to fund AI computational research.
  • AI Computational Services: Funds are used to purchase compute credits from AI providers to run advanced reasoning models on mathematical problems.
  • Research Coordination: We manage the allocation of computational resources across different mathematical problems based on community funding priorities.
  • Public Transparency: All problem attempts, solutions, and fund allocations are publicly visible and verifiable.

About Our Business

Business Model

IndieMath operates as a platform for crowdfunding mathematical research through AI computation. Users contribute funds that are converted into AI compute credits and applied to solving open mathematical problems. All transactions are transparent and verifiable.

Use of Funds

Contributed funds are used exclusively for:

  • Purchasing AI API credits from providers (primarily Anthropic)
  • Computational infrastructure and storage for problem transcripts
  • Platform operations and maintenance

Transparency

All contributions, fund allocations, and computational spending are publicly auditable. Users can track exactly how their contributions are used and view the AI's work on problems they've funded.
